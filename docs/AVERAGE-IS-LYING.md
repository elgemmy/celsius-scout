# The Average Is Lying

`/average` is a focused derived experience powered by the same deterministic
analysis as Celsius Scout. It does not define new thermal metrics.

## Story

1. Present the broad cohort mean as useful but incomplete context.
2. Open the full distribution of local time-weighted means.
3. Keep each location's longest threshold persistence visible beside its mean.
4. Use the real similar-average/different-behavior scout tool to compare two
   profiles that a single number makes look alike.
5. Show the structured tool name and evidence before explaining the contrast.

## Calculation boundary

- `analyzeCohort(demoCohort)` produces all location features and scores.
- `summarizeAverageMasking(analysis)` supplies the broad mean, local mean range,
  endpoint locations, above/below counts, and observed tile-hour share.
- `findSimilarAverageDifferentBehaviorPair(analysis)` selects and explains the
  comparison pair.
- Sorting and percentage positions in the page are presentation transforms only.
  They do not create or expose new analytical claims.

The route labels the bundled Phoenix data as synthetic. All statements remain
descriptive and cohort-relative; the page makes no causal, medical, safety, or
intervention-effect claims.
